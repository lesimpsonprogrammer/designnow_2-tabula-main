# Tabula Design Now — Vision & Brand Kit V1

Status: **Draft — founder direction, in progress.** Do not build features from this brief until the open questions below are answered.

## Who we are

Tabula Design Now (TDN) is the latest addition to the **Momentum Technology Research & Design Co.** brand of companies, alongside Momentum Data Solutions and Cloud Performance Service Manager (CPSM).

TDN provides an **ultra user design experience** for **Individuals** and for **Teams**.

## How it should feel

> "They should feel like they have just gotten off a roller coaster and had a great time."

Read this as two halves of one ride:

- **The thrill:** big moments, motion, reveals, and a "let's go again" payoff.
- **The track:** the structure is so solid and organized that users never worry about it. Clutter and friction get removed so the thrill can land.

Organization serves the thrill. It does not replace it. Calm and minimal is not the goal.

## The big drops

These are the moments that should deliver the rush:

1. Designing your own typography
2. Designing and approving a logo concept
3. Adding a new team member to the group

**First drop to build: creating Version 1 of the Brand Kit.**

### Color Brand

A new concept: **if an organization could be described in one color, based on its look, feel, voice, and presence, what would it be?**

- **Current Color Brand:** TDN generates it from the organization's look, feel, voice, and presence.
- **Target Color Brand:** the client selects the Color Brand they want to reach.
- **The goal:** the client understands **what it takes to get from their current Color Brand to their target.** TDN shows both:
  - **Specific moves for each element** (logo, colors, typography, voice).
  - **A distance meter** that moves closer to the target as moves are made.
