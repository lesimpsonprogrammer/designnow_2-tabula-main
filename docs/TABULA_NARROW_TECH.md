# Tabula Narrow Tech — Design Brief

Tabula Narrow Tech is Tabula Design Now's original condensed technology-focused sans family.

## Personality

- Precise, technical, efficient, modern
- Narrow proportions for dashboards, navigation, labels, dense interfaces, and data products
- Cleaner and more distinctive than a generic condensed system sans
- Functional first, but with recognizable signature glyphs

## Core Construction

- Condensed neo-grotesk structure
- Low stroke contrast
- Subtly squared curves and terminals
- Tight, controlled spacing
- Open-medium counters to preserve readability at smaller sizes
- Strong numerals suitable for tables, metrics, dashboards, and technical labels

## Signature Glyphs

### A
- Narrow apex
- Slightly flattened top point rather than a sharp geometric spike
- Medium-low crossbar

### G
- Open technical G
- Squared inner spur
- Compact aperture

### M
- Straight outer stems
- Deep center vertex
- Narrow overall width

### R
- Compact bowl
- Crisp diagonal leg
- Leg exits slightly early to keep the letter tight

### T
- Signature character
- Slightly taller vertical stem/root than neighboring capitals
- Broad but controlled top bar
- Stem should feel engineered, not decorative

### 1
- Upright stem
- Short angled flag
- Stable foot for legibility in tables

### 4
- Open-top construction
- Crisp triangular counter
- Strong vertical stem

### 7
- Flat top
- Decisive diagonal
- Optional short crossbar alternate for technical/data contexts

### 0
- Narrow oval zero
- Clear distinction from capital O
- Slashed-zero alternate for technical contexts

## Rendering Target in Tabula

Before the final custom font file is installed, Tabula uses a condensed fallback stack and applies a tighter technical rendering profile. The final `.woff2` should eventually carry its proportions, spacing, kerning, tabular numerals, and signature glyph construction natively.

## Commercial Direction

The family should remain original rather than tracing or modifying Arial Narrow. The goal is an Arial-adjacent familiarity in readability and utility while establishing independent proportions, terminals, numerals, and signature glyphs suitable for future licensing as a Tabula type family.
